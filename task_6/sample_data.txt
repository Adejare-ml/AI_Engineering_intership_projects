This is a sample document about the history and evolution of Artificial Intelligence.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems. 
The term 'Artificial Intelligence' was coined by John McCarthy in 1956 during the Dartmouth Conference.
AI has evolved from simple rule-based systems to complex machine learning models and deep learning architectures.
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technique used to enhance the output of a Large Language Model (LLM) by retrieving relevant information from an external knowledge base before generating a response.
This allows the model to provide more accurate and domain-specific answers without needing to be retrained on every new piece of information.
Vector databases, such as ChromaDB and Pinecone, are critical for RAG because they allow for efficient similarity searches using embeddings.
Embeddings are numerical representations of text that capture semantic meaning, allowing the system to find content that is conceptually similar even if the exact words differ.
